# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

The Application Form Management System project is committed to providing a
professional, respectful, and constructive environment for everyone who
participates in the project.

Contributors, maintainers, reviewers, users, and other participants are
expected to communicate professionally and treat one another with respect.

---

## Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and professional.
- Communicate clearly and constructively.
- Consider different technical perspectives.
- Give useful and specific feedback.
- Accept reasonable technical disagreement.
- Focus discussions on the project and its implementation.
- Respect privacy and confidential information.
- Help maintain a welcoming technical environment.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment or personal attacks.
- Threats or intimidation.
- Discriminatory or hateful conduct.
- Deliberate disruption of project discussions.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Sharing credentials, secrets, or confidential information.
- Sexual harassment or unwanted sexual attention.
- Trolling or intentionally abusive communication.
- Maliciously interfering with project infrastructure.
- Retaliation against a person who reports a legitimate concern.

---

## Technical Discussions

Technical disagreement is expected in software development.

Participants may disagree about:

- Architecture
- Programming languages
- Dependencies
- Security controls
- Database design
- UI design
- Performance
- Development workflows

Disagreements should remain focused on technical evidence and project
requirements rather than becoming personal.

---

## Security Discussions

Security reports should be handled responsibly.

Participants should not publicly disclose sensitive vulnerability details,
credentials, private applicant information, or exploitable configuration
information.

Security issues should be reported according to the process described in
`SECURITY.md`.

---

## Privacy

Project participants must take reasonable care when handling personal or
confidential information.

Do not publish:

- Passwords
- API keys
- Authentication tokens
- Database credentials
- Private application records
- Uploaded applicant documents
- Personal information that is not necessary for the discussion

Use sanitized examples when demonstrating problems.

---

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may take appropriate action when this Code of Conduct is
violated.

Possible actions may include:

1. A request to modify inappropriate behavior.
2. Removal of inappropriate content.
3. Temporary restriction from project discussions.
4. Removal from project participation.
5. Reporting serious matters to the appropriate service or authority where
   necessary.

The response will depend on the circumstances and severity of the issue.

---

## Reporting Concerns

Concerns about violations should be reported privately to the project
maintainers through the project's designated contact or security-reporting
channel.

When reporting an incident, provide enough information to understand:

- What happened.
- When it happened.
- Where it happened.
- Which project area was involved.
- Any relevant evidence.

Avoid including unnecessary personal or confidential information.

---

## Good-Faith Participation

Reports and moderation requests should be made in good faith.

Retaliation against someone who makes a legitimate report is not acceptable.

False or deliberately misleading reports intended to harm another participant
may themselves be treated as a violation.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project repositories, issue discussions,
pull requests, documentation, development channels, and other spaces used
for project-related collaboration.

---

## Updates

This document may be updated when project governance or community needs
change.

Participants are expected to follow the current version of this document.
